For each candidate:

State the proposed saving and verify the algebraic/layout contract.
Implement it in an isolated experimental build.
Check exact correctness against GMP and run relevant FLINT tests.
Compare complete multiplication against unmodified FLINT using interleaved measurements.
Retain, revise, or reject it based on evidence.

First establish measurement reliability with an unchanged-versus-unchanged control. Confirm promising results in separate runs over a contiguous operand-size interval, including neighboring sizes, squaring, and unbalanced products.
